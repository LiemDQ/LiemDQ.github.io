I've bitten the bullet and will be moving my long-form essays to Substack. This has frankly probably been overdue for a while now, but I wanted to make use of the admittedly unreasonable amount of time I've spent customizing this website[^1]. 

Ultimately, when I started writing stuff I thought I would be writing about a greater variety of topics, but for the 2 readers of this site, it's clear that a distinctive theme has emerged. You can subscribe to my Substack, [Transforming Plenty](https://transformingplenty.substack.com/), if you're interested in the big-picture "technology" and "abundance" focused stuff.


Anything off-topic like my essay on [design teams](https://liem.ca/posts/design_team/) will remain on this site.
